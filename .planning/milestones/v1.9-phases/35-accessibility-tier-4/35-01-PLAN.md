Use automatic activation (arrow key both selects AND moves focus) per the APG Tabs Automatic Activation example — the panel renders synchronously so there is no latency concern. Do NOT introduce `aria-activedescendant`; roving tabindex is the locked choice (RESEARCH §A11Y-10). Do NOT alter `dialogA11y.ts` focus-trap behavior — the panel is inside the dialog card, so programmatic `.focus()` stays within the trap.
