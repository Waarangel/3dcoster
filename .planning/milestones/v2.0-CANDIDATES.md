## Theme / positioning
SlicePrice3D (our most direct rival) wins on **breadth** but is **overwhelming, confusing, and account-gated**, with a **static** pricing guide. Our wedge for v2.0: **simplicity + dynamic guidance + trust + local-first/offline/no-account.** Don't chase breadth; out-guide and out-simplify.

## Already have (do NOT re-borrow)
- Auto-numbered PDF quotes **with valid-until/expiry** (their quotes have this too — parity, not a gap).
- **Geolocation / region tax resolution** — SlicePrice3D only has a manual sales-tax %. Real win; lean on it in marketing.
- Low-stock badges + "won't finish this print" hint (v1.8).

## Explicitly NOT doing
- Replicating their overhead-cost step (confusing for beginners).
- Cloud accounts / hosted-only features as the *default* (keep local-first; hosted is the optional Pro ceiling).
