<objective>
Make the `Input`, `Textarea`, and `Select` primitives auto-generate an `id` via `useId()` when the consumer doesn't supply one. The consumer continues to be responsible for pairing a `<label htmlFor={someId}>` with `<Input id={someId} ... />` when an explicit pairing is needed, but the primitive guarantees that EVERY rendered form element has an `id` attribute on the DOM — even when the consumer forgets to pass one. This is the foundation for plans 19-04 and 19-05 to retrofit `htmlFor`/`id` pairs in CustomerEditModal + Record Sale form (A11Y-07).

Purpose: Closes the primitive-side half of A11Y-07. The retrofit (adding `htmlFor` to existing `<label>` elements + passing `id` through to the primitive) lives in plans 19-04 / 19-05 because those plans already touch the form modals.

Output: Three surgical primitive edits + one consolidated auto-id test file. No consumer files touched.
</objective>
