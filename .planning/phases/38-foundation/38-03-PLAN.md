<objective>
The heart of Phase 38: replace the two pricing effects with the reducer design
settled in 38-RESEARCH (§FOUND-02), and move all derived memos into
useCostDerivedValues. This closes PERF-11 the right way — derivation driven by
events (user edited X / cost basis changed), never by observing values the
deriver itself writes.

Why an effect could never be fixed: the v1.9 dep-trim failed because
`sellingPrice` in the dep array was LOAD-BEARING (it made the second consecutive
edit to the same field re-derive). Full deps ⇒ double render; trimmed deps ⇒
desync. The reducer dissolves the dilemma: edit actions carry the fresh value in
the payload and derive synchronously in the transition — no dep array on the
edit path at all.
</objective>
