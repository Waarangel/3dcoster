<objective>
CONN-01: when a material's price changes on save in the Asset Library, scan every saved job that used that material at its live price, flag the ones whose margin now falls below the user's global target, and surface them in a toast → review modal with a per-job "Apply suggested price" action.

Purpose: Makers change filament prices and silently lose margin on jobs they already priced. This gives an immediate, non-destructive alert (suggestion-not-mutation, FAIL-03 lineage) so they can reprice on their own terms.

Output: A pure `repriceAlert.ts` scan module + tests, a `RepriceReviewModal`, and the Asset-Library save-handler wiring (toast + re-openable modal).
</objective>
