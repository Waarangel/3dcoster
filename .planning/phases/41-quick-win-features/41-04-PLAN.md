<objective>
CONN-03: let the maker attach 1pc/10pc/50pc (editable) tiered pricing to a quote and generate one branded PDF showing all tiers, riding the existing jsPDF quote engine — no new cost math, no pricing-desync risk.

Purpose: Borrowed from 3DPrintQuote's tiered-quote pattern — customers ask "what about 10 or 50?" and the maker answers in one professional document. Tier math is maker-controlled per-tier discount % (D-06), explicitly NOT cost-amortization-at-quantity, so it needs no equivalence tests and touches no cost model.

Output: A Quantity-variants section in PrintQuoteModal, a tier-table branch in generateQuotePdf, and a tier picker on Convert-to-Sale.
</objective>
