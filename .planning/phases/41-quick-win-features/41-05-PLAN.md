<objective>
LIFE-02: let the maker set their jurisdiction and see a progress bar of gross sale revenue toward their local hobby→business tax threshold (UK £1k trading allowance, DE Kleinunternehmer, AU GST, US badges-of-trade note), with a crossing alert — all local, "not tax advice" throughout.

Purpose: Hobby sellers don't know when they've tipped into "running a business." A conservative, disclaimed progress bar over data they already have gives them a heads-up — deliberately gross revenue (how these thresholds are legally defined), never profit.

Output: A pure `taxThreshold.ts` (jurisdiction-aware year window + threshold table + revenue sum) + tests, a `TaxThresholdCard` in the Insights tab, and a jurisdiction picker in Settings.
</objective>
