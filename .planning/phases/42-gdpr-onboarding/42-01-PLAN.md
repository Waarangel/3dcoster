<objective>
Ship the GDPR-01 consent banner: a themed, opt-in vanilla-cookieconsent v3 banner with equal-weight Accept All / Reject All, a consent-gated Vercel Analytics mount that fires zero network requests before consent, one-click revocation without reload from both the marketing Footer and the in-app Settings modal, and complete removal of analytics from the Tauri desktop build.

Purpose: This is the hard-gate requirement — no analytics may fire before explicit opt-in, and the banner itself must be compliant (equal-weight buttons, one-click reject) or the phase fails its legal purpose (PITFALLS C-04).

</objective>
