<objective>
Let a Pro maker publish a saved quote as a live hosted share link and unpublish it, storing the publish metadata on the local Quote, and pull hosted approve/decline status back into the app on open (PRO-02 + the in-app half of D-07). Free users see the Share button behind a single Pro pitch modal (D-10).

Purpose: This is the maker-side of the hosted-quote loop. It reuses the already-self-contained `Quote.lineItemsSnapshot` (D-17 G4 from v1.2) as the publish payload, adds only additive non-indexed publish metadata to the Quote (matching the project's established no-version-bump precedent for non-indexed fields), and enforces the lapse-safe rule (D-11) by gating entitlement ONLY at publish time.

Output: A working Share/Unpublish flow, a Pro pitch for free users, and in-app status refresh.
</objective>
