<objective>
Give the legal surfaces (PrivacyPage, TermsPage, cookie-consent banner) the dark-brand treatment (D-12) with content byte-identical and all existing regex-guard + GDPR E2E tests staying green — while resolving the D-12-vs-D-02 tension on the globally-mounted consent banner so it is brand-dark on marketing routes yet stays slate on /app.

Purpose: D-12 requires the full brand port to reach legal surfaces, but these carry content locks (processor list, Pro-price terms) and GDPR behavior invariants (equal-weight consent, opt-in gating). The consent banner is a special case: CookieConsentManager is mounted once globally at the main.tsx root (NOT per-route), and vanilla-cookieconsent appends its `#cc-main` DOM directly to document.body — outside the React tree and outside MarketingThemeScope's `.theme-brand-dark` div — so it also renders over the slate /app calculator. Branding it unconditionally would violate D-02 on the highest-traffic surface. This plan brands it via a route-scoped `body.route-marketing #cc-main` override (keyed off the 45-01 body marker) so both D-12 and D-02 hold. Everything here is restyle-only; the guards are the safety net.

Output: Restyled Privacy/Terms pages + route-scoped brand-themed consent banner (slate on /app, brand on marketing), guards green.
</objective>
