Deliver white-label PDF quotes (the private deliverable, D-10): for any Pro user, the quote PDF drops the "Made with 3DCoster" footer and renders the maker's logo + business name in the header; for free/lapsed users nothing changes. The PDF generator stays PURE (no Dexie/UserProfile import — its single-arg-by-value rule enforces D-17 G4), so the branding is INJECTED by the sole caller (`PrintQuoteModal.tsx:324`) as a second argument, mirroring how `usePublishedQuote` already takes `businessName` by injection.
