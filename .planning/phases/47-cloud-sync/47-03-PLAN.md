<objective>
The zero-knowledge crypto layer (D-05/D-06): pure WebCrypto AES-256-GCM encrypt/decrypt of the sync-file bytes, Argon2id key derivation from the user passphrase (`hash-wasm`, lazy WASM), a deterministic per-account salt from `auth.uid()` (resolves the D-06/D-08 tension — no stored salt column needed), a `@zxcvbn-ts`-based passphrase strength check, and a non-extractable CryptoKey cache in IndexedDB (D-06 "typed once per device, cached thereafter"). Zero server round-trips: 100% client-side.

Purpose: This is where the "we can't read your data" promise is made real. Every hard sub-problem here has a standard small solution (RESEARCH Don't-Hand-Roll) — the temptation to hand-roll a lighter cipher/KDF is exactly where the zero-knowledge claim would quietly become false.
Output: `syncCrypto.ts` (pure), `syncKeyCache.ts` (IndexedDB key store), tests, and four installed packages.

Spec-less probe skipped (no requirement IDs to probe — visible recorded skip); must_haves derived goal-backward from D-05/D-06/D-07.
