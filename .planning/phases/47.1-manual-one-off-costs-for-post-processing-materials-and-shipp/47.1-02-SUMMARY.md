# Phase 47.1 Plan 02: Hardened post-processing one-off lines, packaging one-off lines, NEW badge Summary

**Both lists in the founder's ask (Post-Processing Materials and Shipping -> Packaging Materials) now accept validated one-off lines: half-filled rows are rejected with toasts, drafts and synced jobs are sanitized on restore, packaging one-offs flow flat into shipping and true cost and save as `manualPackaging`, and both entry points carry a NEW badge with a11y tests.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~9 min (14:02 to 14:11 local, 18:02 to 18:11 UTC)
- **Tasks:** 3 (all TDD)
- **Files:** 9 changed (1 created, 8 modified); 772 insertions, 30 deletions
- **Commits:** 6 (measured from the plan ledger)

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 (post-processing robustness):** `useCostForm` routes the manualMaterials lazy initializer and the editingJob restore through `sanitizeManualLines`. `handleSaveJob` calls a local `normalizeManualLinesOrToast(lines, section)` after the three existing toasts and before job assembly; failures toast and abort, success writes the normalized array (or `undefined` when empty) into the single `sharedFields` literal.
- **Task 2 (packaging, D-01):** new `manualPackaging` slice mirrors manualMaterials (sanitized init, in `nonPricingFormState` and its deps, unconditional sanitized edit restore, reset in `clearForm`). `useCostDerivedValues` takes an optional `manualPackaging` prop (no default, no new branch) and adds `calculateManualLinesCost(manualPackaging)` to the `packagingCost` memo; `totalShippingCost`, `trueCostForReducer` and `trueCost` are untouched, so the amount is flat and not failure-adjusted. `ShippingSection` gets the "+ One-off cost" button (never disabled), an InfoTooltip, and id-keyed one-off rows at the packaging density. The Carrier Cost input and the existing packaging onChange call-site strings are byte-identical.
- **Task 3 (MC-07):** `manual-cost-lines` key in `features.ts` (2026-10-18), `NewBadge` as an absolute overlay inside `relative inline-flex` wrappers on both buttons, plus component tests for both sections including the first-ever `ShippingSection.test.tsx`.
