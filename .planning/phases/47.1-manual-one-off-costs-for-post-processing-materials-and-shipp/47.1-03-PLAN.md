<objective>
Make every downstream consumer of a saved job consistent with one-off lines (MC-06), and make Duplicate carry them (MC-04, Pitfall 5).
- Code changes: Duplicate, what-if/reprice reconstruction, and the Jobs CSV export.
- Regression locks, test-only: quote shipping pre-fill (D-05), stock ledger, backup round-trip, and sync applyMerge.
Consumers that read only `job.costPerUnit` (My Jobs, break-even pill, sales profit, sales-report PDF, insights scrap cost, quote PDF / hosted quote via the by-value snapshot) already reconcile automatically and need no change (RESEARCH Downstream Consumer Matrix).

This also closes the ROADMAP phrase "flow through quotes/PDFs" per D-05: one-off lines reach quotes and PDFs only through `costPerUnit` and profit. They never render on customer documents and never feed the shipping pre-fill.

Purpose: one-off costs must not silently vanish (Duplicate), be mis-modelled (what-if), be invisible in exports, mint phantom stock, or be lost across devices or backups.
Output: three small production edits plus regression tests, and a test-only fix for a pre-existing wall-clock time-bomb that currently fails `npm run build` (needed for the 47.1-05 phase gate).
