# Phase 47.1 Plan 04: Save Job "Add to your asset library?" prompt Summary

**Saving or updating a job with not-yet-asked one-off lines now opens a pre-save popup (every row defaults to Keep as one-off, Cancel/Escape aborts the save); accepted lines become library assets through the sync-stamped `addAsset` path while the job keeps each line by value, so cost is identical either way and stock is never deducted.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~7 min
- **Tasks:** 2 (both TDD)
- **Files:** 9 changed (2 created, 7 modified)
- **Commits:** 6 (measured from the plan ledger)

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 (helpers + presenter):** `manualCostLines.ts` gained the pure decision module: `promptableLines` (skips already-asked lines, blank names, names already in the line's own picker list, and repeats within a section; post-processing first), `buildLibraryAsset` (unit `ea`, 1 per package, package cost = cost per unit = the typed cost, no stock fields; packaging forced to the Packaging category), `markLinesPrompted` (copy-on-flag, never mutates), and the category constants. `SaveToLibraryModal` is a presenter-only modal on the shared `Modal`/`Select`/`Button` with a reset-on-open effect, a conditional category Select, an inline error on a rejected confirm, and Cancel/Escape ignored while a confirm is pending.
- **Task 2 (orchestrator wiring):** `handleSaveJob` keeps the three validation toasts and the 47.1-02 normalization, then either calls `persistJob` or parks the normalized lists in `libraryPrompt`. `handleLibraryConfirm` awaits `onAddAsset(buildLibraryAsset(...))` sequentially, then flags the form (functional updaters) and persists the flagged lists. `persistJob` holds the existing job assembly and keeps exactly one `taxRate: tax.ratePercent` literal. `App.tsx` passes `onAddAsset={addAsset}`; the golden-scenario fixture mirrors the new prop (no scenario touched). CHANGELOG `[Unreleased]` has the user-facing bullet.
