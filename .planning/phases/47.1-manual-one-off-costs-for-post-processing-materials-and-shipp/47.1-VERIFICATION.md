**Phase Goal:** Users can add manual one-off cost lines (name + cost) to Post-Processing Materials and Shipping without a library asset; on Save Job a popup offers per-item "add to asset library?". Manual lines persist on the job snapshot, flow through quotes/PDFs, and survive cloud sync (Phase 47). Amortization of reusable tools is out of scope.
